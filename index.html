How we decided

Cost per lead
Ad spend ÷ the leads the platform reports (calls and forms). Google leads exclude brand search.
Searches missed for budget
Google's own figure. High means more budget would show the ads more often.
Booked consults
Leads that reached a booked stage in SmileHub: the best sign of real patients.
Cost per booked consult
Ad spend ÷ booked consults. When money moves, it goes to the cheaper platform.

Rules we apply everywhere

  • Money moves to the platform with the cheaper booked consult, when the gap is clear and it can use more budget.
  • With no booking data we decide on cost per lead, searches missed for budget and Meta frequency, and say so.
  • Lead follow-up and disapproved ads are fixed before money is added.
  • Every move is checked after 30 days on cost per booked consult, and rolled back if it misses.

Paid ad calls count as Google; Facebook leads count as Meta.

Booking data covers 37 of 63 accounts

  • Booked counts are a floor: many leads were never worked (Carroll 108 of 176, Smiles 61 of 112).
  • 435 bulk-imported records were removed from Kapil's accounts so they don't flatter cost per booked.
  • The 26 accounts without booking data are judged on Google and Meta data only, and labelled that way.
